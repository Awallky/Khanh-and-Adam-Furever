Vietnam Logistics

Getting to An Lâm Retreats

Nearest Airport:

  • SGN (Tan Son Nhat International Airport, Ho Chi Minh City)

Resort Transfer: An Lâm provides private 25-minute scenic speedboat transfers directly from central Saigon (Bach Dang Pier) or private vehicle transfers.

Passport & Visa: Please verify your passport has at least 6 months validity and check Vietnam e-Visa requirements prior to departure.
